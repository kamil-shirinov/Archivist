            The test is on held-out pages of the US Treasury Bulletin, which no model had seen: 400 cells drawn in advance and labelled by hand from the printed page, never shown a model’s answer. The corrected key differs from the first-pass key only where a check of every first-pass blank on its printed page found a dash, by a rule written down before anyone looked (PREREGISTRATION_REAL.md, Deviations). Wrong among accepted is given against the single read; intervals are 95%, resampled by page. Sending to a person does not depend on the key. The score files are in results/real_test/held_out/ and results/real_openw/held_out/ (score_corrected/ and score/).
